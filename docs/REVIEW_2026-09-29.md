# Review and delivery — 28–29 September 2026

Scope: review the platform, move it to Supabase, and deliver the priority
product changes (pricing, Todo boards, company subdomains, invitations,
deployment). Nothing has been committed, pushed or deployed.

## What the platform is

Nexus is a modular SaaS: 13 Node services (gateway, identity, tenancy, catalog,
billing, audit, notifier, CRM, HR, payroll, tasks, documents, invoicing) and a
Next.js web app. Released apps: CRM, HR, Payroll, Tasks, Documents, Invoicing.
The code quality is high — tenant isolation, permission checks, outbox events
and regression tests are consistently applied.

## Delivered

**Supabase.** One schema per service (`nexus_crm`, `nexus_hr`, …) in the
Supabase database, set as a connection startup parameter. Migrations run on
boot. The event bus now runs on Postgres (`nexus_bus` schema), so no NATS is
needed; consumers work under short leases and hold no connection while
handlers run.

**Pricing** (apps are ₹0 individually):

| Plan | Price | Seats | Apps | Extra seat | Extra app |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | ₹1,599/mo | 10 | any 5 | ₹259/mo | ₹99/mo |
| Business | ₹2,599/mo | 20 | any 5 | ₹259/mo | ₹99/mo |

Plus 18% GST. Annual = 10 months for 12. Every term is invoiced **in advance**;
access follows payment (trial → first invoice due at trial end; renewal invoice
a week before term end; 3-day grace). Seats or apps added mid-term are charged
pro rata. Razorpay Checkout + signature verification + webhook; a local test
mode settles invoices without money. Billing page: pay, seats, plan, cycle,
printable invoices.

**App suggestions.** Picking HR pre-selects Payroll; CRM pre-selects Invoicing;
the customer can untick them and they are not re-added. "Works well with" hints
for related apps; a counter shows 5 included and the ₹99 for each extra.

**Tasks & Boards (Todo).** Boards are private by default. The owner adds
people as owner/editor/viewer — one employee on one board, another on another,
and neither can see, edit or be assigned the other's work (unknown boards
return 404). Owners/admins see every board. Personal to-dos are visible to
their creator and assignee. Monday-style table view (status/priority pills,
inline dates, quick add per group) alongside Kanban; share dialog; people get a
notification when added. Employees (the Employee role) can now use their
boards in the main workspace, with "My HR portal" one click away.

**Company subdomains.** Sign-up asks for the company name; the workspace gets
`<company>-<4 digits>` (e.g. `sharma-traders-6133`). Signing in on the main
site redirects to `https://<slug>.<domain>/…`; a company's sign-in page names
the company; a subdomain you are not a member of shows "no access"; switching
workspace moves to that company's address. Invitation links use the company's
address.

**Invitations.** Email with the invite link; the invitee sees the company, the
invited email is locked, and they add their name, mobile and password.

**Deployment.** `Dockerfile` + `railway.json` (all services in one container,
gateway on `$PORT`), `apps/web/vercel.json`, `/api` rewrite from Vercel to
Railway. See `docs/DEPLOYMENT.md`.

## Open items and recommendations

1. **Rotate the Supabase password** — it was shared in chat.
2. **Supabase free tier** (0.5 GB, 60 connections, pauses after a week idle)
   is fine for development, not for customers. Use Pro with Micro/Small compute.
3. **Latency**: each request makes several database round trips. Keep Railway
   in Singapore (closest to Mumbai). Longer term, a read-through cache for
   authorization and fewer sequential queries per request.
4. Documents are stored on the container disk — attach a Railway volume now;
   move to Supabase Storage/S3 before running more than one API replica.
5. `smoke:*` scripts were updated for the new plans but not re-run in full on
   Supabase (the regression suite was).
6. Local dev port 3000 is used by another app on this machine; this project's
   `.env` uses `WEB_PORT=3100` and the `lvh.me` subdomain settings.

Platform changes made along the way:
- The Postgres event bus now runs each consumer independently. A handler backing off (for example, a failing email) no longer delays in-app notifications in the same process. The full suite runs about 40% faster as a result.
- The notifier treats a mail-provider 5xx as final: it records the failure and does not retry. It never sends to reserved test domains (`.test`, `example.com`, …), so test runs cannot send mail through the real ZeptoMail account.
- `peopleDirectory` (service-kit) gives each app a name list, such as helpdesk agents or interviewers, without granting access to the member directory.
